Bill No. 200 of 2025 · Lok Sabha
The Securities Markets Code, 2025
Regulatory
Consolidates SEBI Act 1992, SCRA 1956, and Depositories Act 1996 into a single unified code. Introduces regulatory sandboxes, enhanced enforcement, Ombudsperson mechanism, and raises maximum penalties to ₹25 crore. Directly impacts all market infrastructure institutions, intermediaries, and listed entities.

Bill No. 187 of 2025 · Rajya Sabha
The Banking Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2025
Neutral
Amends Banking Regulation Act 1949 and RBI Act 1934. Introduces new governance norms for cooperative banks, raises director tenure limits, and enhances RBI's supervisory powers over scheduled banks. Compliance cost impact for mid-size private banks.

Bill No. 193 of 2025 · Lok Sabha
The Electricity (Amendment) Bill, 2025
Bullish
Major amendment to Electricity Act 2003. Enables open access for renewable energy, introduces smart metering mandate for all consumers above 10kW, and creates new regulatory framework for green hydrogen distribution. Positive for renewable energy players and smart grid companies.
